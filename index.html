Sourcing playbook

How to actually buy 10 tables / month at wholesale

At 10 tables/month (~£80–150k/year wholesale spend), you're a serious trade buyer. The strategy is 5–10 deep relationships, not scraping 188 websites. Use this catalogue to build the shortlist, then move to phone and in-person.

① The 12-supplier starter shortlist

Click ⭐ Starter Pack in the header to see them. Six container-scale UK importers, two Ireland-side options, four rustic-accessory wholesalers (for brick moulds, dough bowls, chopping boards), and two administrator alert channels. Contact all 14 in one week.

② Trade shows — the single highest-leverage day

Two days at one trade show = 6 months of phone calls. Dates to put in the calendar now:

  • January Furniture Show — NEC Birmingham, mid-January (the biggie)
  • Spring Fair — NEC Birmingham, early February (giftware + furniture + rustic décor)
  • Top Drawer — Olympia London, mid-January + mid-September (design-led)
  • Autumn Fair — NEC Birmingham, early September
  • INDX Furniture & Flooring — Coventry, trade-only, quarterly

Entry typically £15–30. Most exhibitors have show-only sample pricing + immediate trade-account setup.

③ Warehouse visits > phone calls

Plan one Midlands day trip — you can fit in Hill Interiors (Derbyshire), Coach House (Lancashire), Baumhaus (Bicester), Heartlands (Smethwick), Indian Hub (Corby). Book 1 hour each. This turns an anonymous trade account into a contact who texts you when new containers arrive. At your volume, every importer in this list will say yes to a warehouse visit.

④ Container splits — the real unlock

A full 20ft container from India is £15–25k / ~100–150 pieces. You're already close to a full container quarterly. Ask Millwards, Coach House, or Indian Hub about sharing a container with another retailer — gets you within 10–15% of direct-from-factory pricing. After 2–3 successful container splits, a direct scouting trip to Jodhpur (sheesham, mango, reclaimed) or Jepara (Indonesian teak) is financially viable: ~£2,500 all-in, saves 25–35%.

⑤ Administration / insolvency alerts

This is where the biggest deals happen — before stock ever reaches public auction. Register your trade interest with:

  • FRP Advisory — frpadvisory.com (now in catalogue)
  • Begbies Traynor — begbies-traynorgroup.com (now in catalogue)
  • Interpath Advisory — interpath.com
  • Teneo — teneo.com/services/restructuring

When a furniture retailer enters administration, administrators email their registered buyer list directly. 50–80% off wholesale is common. The auction houses (John Pye, Wilsons) are what's left over after administrators' trade buyers.

⑥ The under-the-radar channels

  • Private Facebook groups: "UK Interiors Trade", "Rustic Furniture Trade UK" — invite-only, ask any existing trade contact for referral
  • LinkedIn DMs to importer owners — surprisingly responsive vs website contact forms
  • WhatsApp broadcast lists — ask to be added after first order; "container arriving Tuesday, first pick" messages
  • INDX + The Retail Association (TRA) membership — private dealer days

Realistic 4-week launch plan

  1. Week 1 — Prep: business/VAT sorted, trade references ready, one-page "about our stores" PDF, dedicated trade@ email.
  2. Week 2 — Apply: trade account applications with all 12 starter-pack suppliers + admin-alert registration with FRP + Begbies. Book travel for the Midlands day.
  3. Week 3 — Visit: Midlands warehouse day (5 importers), attend next trade show (or pre-book ticket + hotel).
  4. Week 4 — Act: first order with the 2–3 importers that felt best. Ask about container splits with the strongest one. Add yourself to 2 private trade Facebook groups.
This catalogue narrows the field from hundreds to the right dozen. The margin verification is still a human task per supplier — but the phone calls become cheap once the shortlist is right.
